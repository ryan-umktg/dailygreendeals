Terpenes are the aromatic compounds that give plants their smell. They're why lemons smell citrusy, pine needles smell like pine, and lavender smells like lavender. Cannabis and hemp produce them in the same resin glands that make cannabinoids like CBD and THC, and each strain's mix of terpenes creates its distinct aroma.

## Common terpenes in hemp and cannabis

| Terpene | Aroma | Also found in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Myrcene** | Earthy, musky, herbal | Hops, mango, lemongrass |
| **Limonene** | Citrus | Citrus peel |
| **Beta-caryophyllene** | Peppery, spicy | Black pepper, cloves |
| **Alpha-pinene** | Pine | Pine needles, rosemary |
| **Linalool** | Floral | Lavender |
| **Humulene** | Woody, earthy | Hops |
| **Terpinolene** | Fruity, floral, piney | Apples, lilac |

Beta-caryophyllene stands out because it can bind to the body's CB2 cannabinoid receptors, which is unusual for a terpene. Researchers are still studying what that means in practice.

## The "entourage effect"

The entourage effect is the idea that cannabinoids and terpenes work better together than separately. It's the main argument for **full-spectrum** and **broad-spectrum** products over isolate. It's a reasonable hypothesis and widely used in marketing, but scientific evidence for it in people is still limited and mixed. Treat strong claims about specific terpenes producing specific effects with caution.

## Terpenes on product labels

You'll see terpenes listed in three main ways:

- **Naturally occurring:** retained from the hemp extract (common in full-spectrum products)
- **Strain-specific or "botanically derived":** terpenes from other plants, blended to recreate a strain's aroma
- **Flavor:** some products simply list "natural flavors"

A complete COA may include a **terpene panel** listing each one, usually in mg/g or %. See [how to read a COA](/guides/how-to-read-a-cbd-coa/).

## Do terpenes matter when buying?

Mostly they matter for **taste and smell**. That's especially true for vapes and flower, where aroma is a big part of the experience. If you prefer a particular flavor profile, the terpene list helps you find it. Choosing terpenes for specific health effects isn't supported by strong evidence.

Terpenes are concentrated plant oils. In vapes, choose products made for inhalation with lab-tested ingredients ([vape guide](/guides/cbd-vape-oil/)).
