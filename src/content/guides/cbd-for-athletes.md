Many athletes use CBD as part of their routine. If you compete under drug-testing rules, the main question isn't whether CBD is allowed. It's whether the product you buy contains anything that isn't.

## What anti-doping rules say

- **CBD itself:** The World Anti-Doping Agency (WADA) removed cannabidiol from its prohibited list in 2018. Many sports that follow the WADA Code allow it.
- **Other cannabinoids:** THC and most other cannabinoids remain **prohibited in competition** under the WADA Code.
- **Strict liability:** Under anti-doping rules, you're responsible for anything found in your sample, whatever the label said.

College, professional and amateur leagues set their own rules, and those rules change. Check your governing body's current list before using any cannabinoid product.

## The real risk: THC you didn't expect

Hemp products aren't reviewed by the FDA before sale, and independent testing has found products with more THC than their labels state. Full-spectrum products contain THC by design. To lower the risk:

1. Choose **CBD isolate** products, or broad spectrum if your sport allows it.
2. Check that the **batch-specific COA** shows THC and other cannabinoids as "ND" ([how to read a COA](/guides/how-to-read-a-cbd-coa/)).
3. Look for products certified by a **third-party sport testing program** where available.
4. Keep the packaging and batch numbers of anything you use.

Our [drug-testing guide](/guides/does-cbd-show-up-on-a-drug-test/) covers this in more detail.

## Vegan-friendly CBD

If you follow a plant-based diet, the CBD itself is plant-derived. Check the other ingredients:

- **Gummies:** many use **gelatin**. Look for **pectin-based** gummies labeled vegan.
- **Capsules:** softgels are often gelatin. Look for **vegetable cellulose (HPMC)** capsules.
- **Oils:** usually vegan (MCT, hemp seed or olive oil carriers), but check the flavorings.
- **Topicals:** some balms contain **beeswax** or lanolin.

A note on nutrition: hemp **seeds** are a good source of protein and omega fatty acids, but CBD extracts are taken in such small amounts that they add no meaningful nutrition. Be wary of products that claim otherwise.

## Talk to your team

Team physicians and athletic trainers know your sport's rules and your health history. Check with them before adding any supplement, especially if you take prescription medication, since CBD can interact with some drugs.
