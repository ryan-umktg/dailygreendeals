Gummies are the best-selling CBD format, and it's easy to see why: no measuring, no earthy taste, and each piece has a set amount. Chocolates, mints, baked goods, drink mixes and capsules work the same way, since you swallow and digest them.

## Why people choose edibles

- **Fixed servings.** Each gummy or capsule contains a stated amount, usually 10–25 mg of CBD.
- **Taste.** Flavors cover the grassy taste of hemp extract.
- **Discretion and convenience.** They're easy to carry and take on a schedule.
- **Longer duration.** Digested CBD is released more slowly than inhaled or sublingual CBD.

## The trade-offs

- **They're slow.** Effects typically take 30 minutes to 2 hours to be noticed. Don't take more because you don't feel anything after 20 minutes.
- **Less reaches the bloodstream.** Swallowed CBD is partly broken down before it gets there. Taking edibles with food helps.
- **Sugar and additives.** A daily gummy habit adds up. Sugar-free and vegan (pectin-based) options exist.

## Reading the label

1. **mg per piece vs. per package.** "750 mg" on the front is usually the whole jar. Divide by the count: 750 mg ÷ 30 gummies = 25 mg each.
2. **Extract type.** Full spectrum (contains some THC), broad spectrum (THC removed) or isolate (CBD only).
3. **Other cannabinoids.** Many gummies add CBN, CBG or melatonin. Check what you're actually buying.
4. **The COA.** It should match the batch and show CBD close to the label ([how to read one](/guides/how-to-read-a-cbd-coa/)).

### THC in gummies is changing

From **December 11, 2026** (unless delayed), finished hemp products may contain no more than **0.4 mg total THC per container**, meaning the whole jar. Most full-spectrum gummies and all hemp "THC gummies" sold until now exceed that. Expect reformulated products, and check the COA rather than older product listings.

## Safety

- **Keep edibles away from children and pets.** They look like candy. Store them in their original packaging, out of reach.
- **Medication interactions.** CBD can affect how some prescription drugs are processed. Ask a doctor or pharmacist first if you take any medication or are pregnant or breastfeeding.
- **Start low.** Begin with one piece, or half, and wait a few hours before deciding whether to take more.

## Comparing prices

The fair way to compare edible deals is **cost per mg of CBD**: sale price ÷ total mg in the package. A cheaper jar with smaller gummies often costs more per mg.
