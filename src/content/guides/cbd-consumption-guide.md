CBD comes in more formats than almost any other supplement. They differ in how you take them, how quickly people notice them, how long the effects last, and how easy it is to know exactly how much you're getting. Here's the overview.

## Swallowed: gummies, capsules, edibles and drinks

**How it works:** CBD is digested and processed by the liver before it reaches the bloodstream. A lot of it is lost along the way. Taking it with a meal, especially a fatty one, helps.

- **Typically felt in:** 30 minutes to 2 hours
- **Lasts:** the longest of any format
- **Good for:** fixed, consistent servings and convenience
- **Watch for:** sugar content, gelatin (if you're vegan), and how mg is listed (per piece vs. per package)

More in our [guide to CBD edibles](/guides/cbd-edibles/).

## Under the tongue: oils and tinctures

**How it works:** you hold drops under your tongue so some CBD absorbs through the mouth, then swallow the rest.

- **Typically felt in:** 15–60 minutes
- **Good for:** adjusting your serving in small steps
- **Watch for:** working out mg per dropper (see our [tincture guide](/guides/cbd-tinctures/))

## Inhaled: vapes and hemp flower

**How it works:** CBD is absorbed through the lungs, the fastest route.

- **Typically felt in:** minutes
- **Lasts:** shorter than swallowed CBD
- **Watch for:** only use products made for vaping, and buy from brands with full lab reports. Read our [vape guide](/guides/cbd-vape-oil/) and [hemp flower guide](/guides/smokable-cbd-flower/) first.

Inhaling anything has lung-health trade-offs, and many states restrict vape and smokable hemp products.

## On the skin: topicals and patches

**How it works:** creams, balms and lotions act on the area where they're applied and aren't designed to reach the bloodstream. Transdermal patches are formulated to pass through the skin over several hours.

- **Good for:** targeted use and people who don't want to swallow or inhale anything
- **Watch for:** the actual CBD amount per container. Some lotions contain very little.

## Choosing a format

| If you want… | Consider |
| --- | --- |
| The simplest fixed serving | Gummies or capsules |
| Fine control over the amount | An oil or tincture |
| Something fast | A vape (with care) |
| Something for one area | A topical |
| CBD in a drink | A water-soluble product ([nano CBD explained](/guides/nanoenhanced-hemp-oil/)) |

## Whatever you choose

- Check the batch **COA** ([how to read one](/guides/how-to-read-a-cbd-coa/)).
- Start with a low serving.
- Talk to a doctor or pharmacist if you take prescription medication, since CBD can interact with some drugs.
- Keep cannabis products, especially edibles, away from children and pets.

Hemp-derived CBD products are not FDA-approved, and nothing on this page is medical advice.
