The dropper bottle is the classic CBD product. Most of what's sold as a "tincture" today is technically **CBD oil**: CBD extract mixed into a carrier oil, usually MCT (coconut) oil, sometimes hemp seed or olive oil. A true tincture uses alcohol or glycerin as the base. Either way, you use them the same way.

## How to take a tincture

1. Shake the bottle.
2. Fill the dropper to your serving size. Many droppers are marked, and a full dropper is usually about 1 mL.
3. Hold the oil under your tongue for 30–90 seconds, then swallow.

Holding it under your tongue (sublingual use) lets some CBD absorb through the lining of the mouth. In practice much of it is swallowed and digested like an edible. That's why people's experience of how quickly it works and how long it lasts varies a lot.

## Working out your serving

Labels list total CBD in the bottle. To get mg per mL:

> **Total mg ÷ bottle size in mL = mg per mL**

For example, 1,500 mg in a 30 mL bottle is 50 mg per mL, so half a dropper (0.5 mL) is about 25 mg. Brands suggest a starting serving on the label. The common advice is to start low and only increase gradually.

This math is also the easiest way to **compare deals**. Divide the sale price by total mg of CBD to get the cost per mg.

## Tinctures vs. other formats

| Format | Typically felt | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tincture / oil | 15–60 min | Easy to adjust the serving; taste can be earthy |
| Gummies & capsules | 30 min–2 hrs | Fixed serving per piece; more lost in digestion |
| Vapes | Minutes | Fastest; harder to measure; see our [vape guide](/guides/cbd-vape-oil/) |
| Topicals | Applied to skin | Not designed to reach the bloodstream |

Research on how much CBD actually reaches the bloodstream is limited. Swallowed CBD has low and variable absorption, and taking it with food, especially fatty food, raises it noticeably.

## Full spectrum, broad spectrum or isolate?

- **Full spectrum:** the whole range of hemp compounds, including a little THC.
- **Broad spectrum:** the same idea with the THC removed.
- **Isolate:** CBD only.

Federal rules taking effect **December 11, 2026** cap finished hemp products at 0.4 mg total THC per container. Many full-spectrum tinctures were well above that, so formulas and availability are changing.

## Before you buy

- Find the batch-specific **COA** and check the CBD and THC numbers ([how to read one](/guides/how-to-read-a-cbd-coa/)).
- Check the carrier oil if you have allergies (coconut, for example).
- Check storage: keep it cool and dark. Most oils last 12–24 months unopened.

## Side effects and interactions

CBD is generally well tolerated, but reported side effects include drowsiness, diarrhea and changes in appetite. It can also interact with prescription drugs, including blood thinners and some seizure medications. Talk to a doctor or pharmacist first if you take any medication or are pregnant or breastfeeding.
