CBD hemp flower is the dried bud of hemp plants bred for high CBD. It looks and smells like marijuana and is sold loose or as pre-rolls. Here's what to know before buying it, especially now that the federal rules are changing.

## Hemp flower vs. marijuana

Hemp and marijuana are the same plant species, *Cannabis sativa*. The legal difference has always been THC content:

- **Until now:** hemp was defined as no more than **0.3% Δ9-THC** by dry weight. Flower could contain lots of **THCA**, which turns into THC when heated, and still be sold as hemp. That's how high-THCA "hemp flower" became widely sold.
- **From December 11, 2026** (unless Congress delays it again): hemp is measured by **total THC**, including THCA, and finished products are capped at **0.4 mg total THC per container**.

In practice, THCA flower will no longer be legal hemp. **High-CBD flower is affected too**, because even low-THC flower usually contains more than 0.4 mg of total THC in a typical package. Expect hemp flower to become much harder to find online after the deadline. Many states already restrict smokable hemp on their own.

## If you buy hemp flower

1. **Check the COA for the batch**, especially **Total THC** (Δ9-THC + THCA × 0.877). See [how to read a COA](/guides/how-to-read-a-cbd-coa/).
2. **Check your state's rules.** Smokable hemp is banned or restricted in several states.
3. **Keep the packaging and receipt.** Hemp flower is visually identical to marijuana, and field tests may not tell them apart.
4. **Expect it to show up on a drug test.** Hemp flower contains THCA, which becomes THC when smoked ([drug-testing guide](/guides/does-cbd-show-up-on-a-drug-test/)).

## Smoking vs. vaporizing flower

Smoking anything produces combustion byproducts that irritate the lungs. A **dry-herb vaporizer** heats flower below burning temperature, which produces fewer of those byproducts. Neither is risk-free. Holding smoke or vapor in longer doesn't increase absorption, so short, gentle draws are better.

## Alternatives

If flower isn't available or legal where you live, you can get similar plant compounds without smoking from **full-spectrum oils**, where still sold, or **terpene-rich vapes and oils**. Read about [terpenes](/guides/terpenes/) or compare [ways to take CBD](/guides/cbd-consumption-guide/).
